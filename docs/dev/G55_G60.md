## Invariants and deviation conditions

- Do not weaken required language forms to match missing implementation. Preserve explicit Unsupported boundaries for P36, G78 and other separate foundations; no new milestone implementation, NativeAOT or draft edits is authorized by this handoff.

- Bind known syntax in its correct local context. Keep exact bound Contract references and canonical live Types/Origins across edits. Do not reinstate the removed function-wide missing-Origin heuristic: it hid an independently invalid lifetime.
